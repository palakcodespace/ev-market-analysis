# Project Demonstration Video Script
## Title: Electric Vehicle Market Analysis and Intelligence System
**Presenter:** Capstone Student / Senior Analytics Engineer  
**Target Audience:** Course Evaluators, Automotive Executives, Data Analysts  
**Estimated Run Time:** 8–9 minutes

### Demonstration Timeline & Cue Sheet

| Timestamp | Phase | On-Screen Action | Spoken Narration (Verbatim Script) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0:00 – 1:00** | **1. Introduction & Objectives** | Display Title Slide and navigate to Web App Home (`/`). Point to system status ribbon. | "Hello everyone, and welcome to the presentation of my Skill Wallet capstone project: the Electric Vehicle Market Analysis and Intelligence System. The global automotive industry is undergoing the most profound transition in a century. However, decision-makers—such as product directors, investment managers, and sustainability policy researchers—face fragmented data, inconsistent battery metrics, and poor visibility into market dynamics. To solve this, I built an end-to-end analytics platform integrating a 3NF normalized SQL database, automated Python data pipelines, Tableau executive dashboards and stories, and a fully interactive Flask web interface. As shown on screen, the system is fully operational and connected directly to our relational database engine." |
| **1:00 – 2:00** | **2. Dataset Ingestion & Lineage** | Open `docs/data_dictionary.md` and navigate to the `/methodology` page on the web app. | "Let's examine our data lineage. Rather than relying on fabricated statistics, this project aggregates authentic data from the International Energy Agency's Global EV Data Explorer under Creative Commons licensing, combined with real-world technical specifications from the EV-Database repository and verified automotive customer satisfaction benchmarks. Our dataset captures 65 production EV models spanning 23 global manufacturers and 294 historical sales observations covering 2018 through 2024. All continuous and categorical attributes are meticulously documented in our data dictionary with defined units and source constraints." |
| **2:00 – 3:00** | **3. Data Cleaning & Feature Engineering** | Open terminal, run `python src/data_cleaning.py`, and inspect `data/processed/data_cleaning_log.json`. | "Data quality was enforced using a repeatable Python pipeline. In `src/data_cleaning.py`, we execute automated deduplication on composite keys, standardize manufacturer nomenclature—such as mapping 'VW' to 'Volkswagen'—and normalize battery chemistries into LFP, NMC, and NCA. Using Tukey's Interquartile Range method, statistical outliers were analyzed; high-capacity vehicles like electric pickups were preserved as legitimate engineering records rather than discarded. In `src/feature_engineering.py`, we compute crucial domain metrics, including price-to-range economic ratios, energy efficiency in kWh per 100 km, and year-over-year sales growth." |
| **3:00 – 4:30** | **4. SQL Database Architecture & Operations** | Open `database/schema.sql`, show the Mermaid ERD in `docs/database_design.md`, and execute `sql/analysis_queries.sql` in terminal. | "At the core of the system is `ev_market_db`, a 3NF relational database. As seen in the ER diagram, the schema separates manufacturers, battery technologies, geographies, vehicles, customer ratings, and sales. Referential integrity is strictly enforced with foreign keys and check constraints. In `sql/analysis_queries.sql`, I implemented 15 comprehensive SQL operations, including Common Table Expressions, Window Functions—such as DENSE_RANK for annual market share—and CASE statements for range segmentation. Pre-computed SQL views, like `vw_annual_sales_trends`, allow our presentation layer to execute complex queries with sub-millisecond latency." |
| **4:30 – 6:00** | **5. Interactive Executive Dashboard Demo** | Navigate to `/dashboard`. Adjust Year filter to 2024, select 'Tesla', then 'BYD', then click Reset. | "Now, let’s explore the live Executive Dashboard. Across the top, our KPI cards dynamically aggregate over 23.9 million cumulative vehicle sales, 16 active OEMs, an average fleet driving range of 517 km, and an average battery pack capacity of 81.9 kWh. Below, our dual-axis sales trend reveals the post-pandemic inflection in 2021 where sales surged by +94% year-over-year. Notice how adjusting the filter dropdown instantly updates our Plotly charts via asynchronous REST calls to our SQL backend without reloading the page. In the battery scatter matrix, hover tooltips reveal exact model pricing, range, and efficiency metrics." |
| **6:00 – 7:30** | **6. Five-Scene Tableau Story Walkthrough** | Navigate to `/story`. Step through Scenes 1 to 5 sequentially. | "Next, we transition to our Tableau Story, titled 'The Global EV Transition Narrative'. In Scene 1, we establish global market sizing, tracking the expansion from 2.1 million units in 2018 to over 17 million in 2024. Scene 2 breaks down the competitive duopoly between Tesla and BYD, who collectively control over 40% of the world's market. Scene 3 demonstrates consumer category preference, where electric SUVs command over 52% market share. Scene 4 explores the battery technology revolution, illustrating how cobalt-free Lithium Iron Phosphate, or LFP, has become the dominant chemistry for mass-market affordability. Finally, Scene 5 highlights geographic manufacturing concentration and the strategic necessity of localized gigafactory supply chains." |
| **7:30 – 8:30** | **7. Automated Testing & Performance Evaluation** | Open terminal, run `pytest tests/`, and show `docs/testing_report.md`. | "System reliability is guaranteed through automated testing. Running pytest across our four test modules executes 17 test cases—covering database connectivity, referential integrity, data pipeline transforms, analytics calculations, and Flask HTTP routes. All 17 tests pass in 3.8 seconds with 100% test coverage. Our performance benchmark report confirms an average SQL query response time of just 0.55 milliseconds, an average web page render time of 4.4 milliseconds, and a lightweight memory footprint of 42 MB RAM." |
| **8:30 – 9:30** | **8. Key Findings, Limitations & Conclusion** | Return to Overview page (`/`). Summarize findings and conclude. | "In conclusion, this project delivers an authentic, reproducible, and enterprise-grade market intelligence platform. Our key findings confirm that vertical integration in battery cells and software is the primary differentiator for automotive profitability. Future scope includes integrating real-time DC fast-charging telemetry and predictive battery degradation models using machine learning. Thank you for your time, and I welcome any questions regarding the database schema, analytical models, or web implementation." |
