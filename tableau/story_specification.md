# Tableau Story Specification: The Global EV Transition Narrative
## Five-Scene Evidence-Based Executive Story

This document specifies the exact construction, visual sheets, filter configurations, narrative text, and key takeaways for each scene in the **Tableau Story**.

---

### Scene 1: EV Market Overview — The Global Electric Inflection
- **Scene Tab Title:** `1. Market Overview`
- **Primary Visualization:** Dual-Axis Sales Growth & Volume Trend (2018–2024)
- **Active Filter State:** All Manufacturers, All Categories, Years 2018–2024
- **Narrative Callout Box:**
  > Between 2018 and 2024, the global automotive market witnessed an unprecedented technological substitution. Total electric vehicle sales accelerated from 2.1 million units to over 17 million units annually, achieving a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of over 40%. The decisive inflection occurred in 2021, when post-pandemic EV sales surged by +94% year-over-year, driven by dedicated EV platforms, national purchase subsidies, and major OEM electrification commitments.
- **Key Takeaway:** Electric mobility has permanently transcended early-adopter luxury status and is now an established mass-market volume category.

---

### Scene 2: Manufacturer Competition — The Duopoly & Emerging Challengers
- **Scene Tab Title:** `2. OEM Competition`
- **Primary Visualization:** Top 10 Manufacturer Sales Ranking & Market Share Horizontal Bar Chart (2024)
- **Active Filter State:** Sales Year = 2024
- **Narrative Callout Box:**
  > A strategic duopoly has emerged at the summit of the global EV hierarchy: **BYD** and **Tesla** collectively control over 40% of the world's battery-electric vehicle sales. BYD leads in total unit volume (2.85M units in 2024) through aggressive vertical integration across battery cell chemistry (LFP Blade) and semiconductor fabrication. Tesla maintains high software brand equity and global distribution. Meanwhile, European legacy stalwarts (Volkswagen, BMW, Mercedes-Benz) and Korean pioneers (Hyundai-Kia) are mounting robust counter-offensives centered on premium styling and ultra-fast 800V charging.
- **Key Takeaway:** Scale and vertical integration in battery manufacturing are the single greatest determinants of OEM volume leadership.

---

### Scene 3: Vehicle Categories — The Hegemony of Electric SUVs
- **Scene Tab Title:** `3. Category Dynamics`
- **Primary Visualization:** Vehicle Category Sales Donut Chart + Category Range & Price Comparison Table
- **Active Filter State:** Sales Year = 2024
- **Narrative Callout Box:**
  > SUVs and Crossovers have established absolute hegemony in the EV market, capturing over 52% of total global sales volume. The architectural packaging of SUVs naturally accommodates larger battery packs (75–105 kWh) beneath the passenger floor without sacrificing cabin legroom. However, aerodynamic sedans remain the technical efficiency leaders, achieving lower energy consumption per 100 km and higher range-to-capacity ratios due to lower drag coefficients.
- **Key Takeaway:** Automotive product planners must prioritize electric SUV and crossover form factors while mitigating aerodynamic drag to optimize battery pack costs.

---

### Scene 4: Battery Technology & Driving Range — The LFP Revolution
- **Scene Tab Title:** `4. Battery & Range`
- **Primary Visualization:** Battery Capacity vs Driving Range Scatter Matrix + Chemistry Adoption Breakdown
- **Active Filter State:** All 65 Analyzed Vehicle Models
- **Narrative Callout Box:**
  > While Nickel Manganese Cobalt (NMC) continues to power long-range premium vehicles (delivering 500–650 km WLTP range with higher gravimetric energy density of 250 Wh/kg), Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP) has sparked an industrial revolution in mass-market EVs. By completely eliminating ethical and price-volatile cobalt and nickel, LFP reduces cell costs by 20–30% while offering superior thermal runaway safety and longer cycle lifetimes. Standard-range trims across Tesla, BYD, and Ford now universally deploy LFP packs.
- **Key Takeaway:** Dual-chemistry platform strategies (LFP for entry/standard range and NMC for high-performance/long range) represent the optimal industry paradigm.

---

### Scene 5: Geographical Distribution & Strategic Imperatives
- **Scene Tab Title:** `5. Strategic Outlook`
- **Primary Visualization:** Country of Manufacture Distribution Chart + Regional Value Matrix
- **Active Filter State:** Global Manufacturing Data
- **Narrative Callout Box:**
  > The geographic distribution of vehicle manufacturing underscores severe supply chain concentration. China produces over 40% of the discrete EV models analyzed and controls over 60% of critical mineral refining. In response, western industrial policies (e.g., the US Inflation Reduction Act and European local-content regulations) are driving massive capital expenditures into domestic gigafactories. To ensure long-term competitiveness, automotive leaders must secure localized cell supplies, build consumer trust through reliable public DC fast-charging networks, and optimize price-to-range value ratios below $100/km.
- **Key Takeaway:** Supply chain resilience, localized battery gigafactories, and accessible pricing are the non-negotiable pillars for winning the next decade of automotive electrification.
