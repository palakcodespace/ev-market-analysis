Data Cleaning & Preparation Protocol
  • Deduplication: Unique identification on composite keys (manufacturer, model_name, model_year) and vehicle_id.
  • Missing Value Imputation: Numeric nulls in continuous attributes (e.g. charging power) imputed with segment medians to prevent sample bias.
  • Outlier Preservation: Detected statistical outliers using Tukey’s Interquartile Range (IQR = Q3 - Q1). Flagged and verified high-capacity pickup trucks (e.g., Rivian R1T 135 kWh, Ford Lightning 131 kWh) as valid production models rather than errors.
  • Standardization: Mapped manufacturer aliases (e.g. "VW" → "Volkswagen", "MB" → "Mercedes-Benz") and battery chemistries ("LiFePO4" → "LFP").
Mathematical Formulations & Metrics
1. Year-over-Year (YoY) Sales Growth Rate:
YoY Growth (%) = ((Sales_{Year t} - Sales_{Year t-1}) / Sales_{Year t-1}) * 100
2. Manufacturer Market Share:
Market Share (%) = (Manufacturer Annual Units / Total Annual Global Units) * 100
3. Price-to-Range Economic Value Ratio:
Price/Range ($/km) = Base MSRP (USD) / Electric Driving Range (km)
4. Fast-Charging Speed Rate (Range Added per Minute):
Charging Rate (km/min) = (Electric Range (km) * 0.70) / (Fast Charge Time (hrs) * 60)

Data Limitations & Academic Boundary Conditions

In compliance with rigorous data science ethics, the following boundary conditions are formally acknowledged:

  • Separation of Sales vs Registrations: In certain municipal jurisdictions, registration numbers lag wholesale factory deliveries. Figures are anchored to compiled IEA annual statistics.
  • WLTP vs EPA Test Cycles: European WLTP range values typically exceed North American EPA ratings by 10–15% due to differing dynamometer acceleration profiles; standardized conversions are applied where appropriate.
  • Customer Ratings Benchmark: Individual owner survey results represent aggregated consumer indices rather than exhaustive laboratory longevity testing.
